NCS TELCO+ TRAVEL AND REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Document ID: NCS-HR-POL-04 | Version: 2.1 | Effective Date: 1 March 2026
Classification: Internal HR Policy (Training Sample Data)

1. PURPOSE
This policy governs travel booking, expense limits, and the reimbursement
process for NCS Telco+ employees travelling for client site visits, training,
or company business.

2. CLIENT-SITE TRAVEL EXPENSE LIMITS
For domestic client-site travel, employees may claim reimbursement up to a
maximum of ₹4,000 per day, covering local transport, meals, and incidentals
combined. This limit excludes pre-booked flights, trains, and hotel
accommodation, which are booked centrally through the Travel Desk and are
not part of the employee's own reimbursement claim.

3. HOTEL AND FLIGHT BOOKING
All flights and hotels for official travel must be booked through the
NCS Travel Desk at least 5 working days in advance, except in genuine
last-minute client emergencies, which require reporting manager sign-off
via email. Employees should not book flights or hotels independently and
seek reimbursement afterward, except in pre-approved exceptional cases.

4. LOCAL CONVEYANCE (NON-CLIENT-SITE)
For local travel within the employee's home city for company business
(e.g., attending an internal event), local conveyance may be claimed up to
₹500 per day, with receipts required for any single expense above ₹200.

5. SUBMISSION PROCESS AND TIMELINES
All reimbursement claims must be submitted through the Expense Portal
within 15 calendar days of the expense being incurred, with scanned
receipts attached for every line item above ₹200. Claims submitted after
30 days will not be processed except with HR Head approval for
documented exceptional circumstances.

6. APPROVAL WORKFLOW
Claims up to ₹10,000 require only reporting manager approval. Claims above
₹10,000 require both reporting manager and department head approval.
Approved claims are typically reimbursed within 10 working days via
salary account transfer.

7. INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL
International travel expense limits and per-diem rates are handled under a
separate International Travel Addendum and are not covered by the ₹4,000
per day domestic limit referenced above.

8. NON-REIMBURSABLE ITEMS
Alcoholic beverages, entertainment expenses, personal shopping, and traffic
or parking fines are never reimbursable under any circumstance.

9. CONTACT
For questions on claim status or policy exceptions, contact the Finance &
Expense Helpdesk, not the general HR Helpdesk.
