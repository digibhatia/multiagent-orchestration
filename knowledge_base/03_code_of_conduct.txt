NCS TELCO+ CODE OF CONDUCT AND ANTI-HARASSMENT POLICY
Document ID: NCS-HR-POL-03 | Version: 3.0 | Effective Date: 1 February 2026
Classification: Internal HR Policy (Training Sample Data)

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets expectations for professional conduct and defines the
process for reporting harassment, discrimination, or workplace misconduct
of any kind, in line with the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act.

2. EXPECTED CONDUCT
All employees, contractors, and vendors representing NCS Telco+ are expected
to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and professionalism,
free from discrimination based on gender, religion, caste, disability,
sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic.

3. REPORTING A HARASSMENT COMPLAINT
Any employee who experiences or witnesses harassment should report it
through ONE of the following channels, whichever feels most comfortable:
(a) Directly to the Internal Committee (IC) at ic.complaints@ncstelcoplus-
sample.com (fictional address for training purposes), (b) to their HR
Business Partner, or (c) through the anonymous ethics hotline listed on
the HR portal. Employees are NOT required to report to their own reporting
manager first if the complaint involves that manager or if they are
uncomfortable doing so.

4. INTERNAL COMMITTEE (IC) PROCESS
All complaints are acknowledged within 2 working days and a formal inquiry
is initiated within 7 working days, led by the Internal Committee, which
includes an external member as required by law. The complainant and
respondent are both given an opportunity to be heard. The IC aims to
complete its inquiry and submit findings within 90 days of the complaint
being filed, as mandated under the POSH Act.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-RETALIATION
All complaints are handled with strict confidentiality. Retaliation against
anyone who files a complaint in good faith, or who participates in an
investigation as a witness, is itself a serious violation of this policy
and subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

6. DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS
Substantiated violations may result in a written warning, mandatory
training, suspension, or termination, depending on severity, as determined
by the IC's recommendation and HR Head approval.

7. FALSE COMPLAINTS
Complaints made in good faith but not ultimately substantiated do NOT
result in any action against the complainant. However, complaints proven
to have been filed maliciously and knowingly false are themselves subject
to disciplinary action.

8. CONTACT
Questions about this policy that are not urgent may be directed to the HR
Helpdesk. Urgent harassment concerns should go directly to the Internal
Committee contact above, not the general helpdesk.
