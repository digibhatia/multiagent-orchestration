NCS TELCO+ IT HELPDESK & ACCESS POLICY
Document ID: NCS-IT-POL-06 | Version: 1.8 | Effective Date: 1 May 2026
Classification: Internal IT Policy (Training Sample Data)

1. PURPOSE
This policy explains how employees get IT support, reset passwords and
request access to systems.

2. RAISING A TICKET
2.1 All IT issues must be raised through the IT Service Portal or by emailing
the IT helpdesk. Phone support is available only for Priority 1 issues
(complete loss of network or email access).
2.2 Target response times: Priority 1 within 30 minutes, Priority 2 within 4
working hours, Priority 3 within 2 working days.

3. PASSWORDS
3.1 Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 90 days.
3.2 Passwords can be reset by the employee through the self-service portal
after multi-factor verification. If MFA is lost, the employee must raise a
Priority 2 ticket with manager approval.
3.3 Passwords must never be shared, written down or stored in plain text files.

4. VPN AND REMOTE ACCESS
4.1 VPN is mandatory when accessing internal systems from outside an NCS
Telco+ office. Split tunnelling is not permitted.
4.2 VPN access is granted automatically on joining. A lost or reset VPN
token needs a Priority 3 ticket.

5. SOFTWARE REQUESTS
5.1 Only software from the approved catalogue may be installed. Requests for
other software need security review and take up to 5 working days.
