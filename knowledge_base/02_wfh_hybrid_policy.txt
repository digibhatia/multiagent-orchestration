NCS TELCO+ WORK-FROM-HOME AND HYBRID WORK POLICY
Document ID: NCS-HR-POL-02 | Version: 1.4 | Effective Date: 1 January 2026
Classification: Internal HR Policy (Training Sample Data)

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines eligibility and rules for hybrid and remote working
arrangements across NCS Telco+ engineering, delivery, and support teams.

2. STANDARD HYBRID MODEL
All employees in eligible roles follow a hybrid model of 3 days in-office
and 2 days work-from-home per week. In-office days are Tuesday, Wednesday,
and Thursday by default, unless a project-specific schedule is agreed with
the reporting manager and communicated to the team in advance.

3. FULL-TIME REMOTE WORK
Full-time (5 days/week) remote work is NOT available as a standard option.
Exceptions may be granted only for: (a) documented medical requirements
verified by HR, (b) employees relocating for a spouse's employment with
manager and HR Head approval, or (c) roles explicitly designated as
"remote-first" at the time of hiring. All exceptions must be reviewed
every 6 months.

4. NEW JOINEE REQUIREMENT
Employees within their first 3 months of joining (probation period) are
required to work from office 4 days per week, to support onboarding,
mentorship, and team integration. The standard 3-day hybrid model applies
from month 4 onward, subject to satisfactory probation review.

5. EQUIPMENT AND INFRASTRUCTURE
NCS Telco+ provides a laptop and a one-time home-office setup allowance of
₹15,000 for employees in eligible hybrid roles, claimable through the
Travel & Reimbursement process within the first 60 days of eligibility.
A minimum internet speed of 50 Mbps is required for remote work days;
employees are responsible for their own internet connectivity.

6. CORE COLLABORATION HOURS
Regardless of location, all employees must be available and responsive
between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM IST on any working day, to support
cross-team collaboration and client calls.

7. MANAGER DISCRETION
Reporting managers may require additional in-office days during critical
project phases (e.g., go-live weeks, client audits), with at least 3
working days' notice to the team.

8. CONTACT
For hybrid work eligibility questions, contact your reporting manager first,
then escalate to HR Business Partner if unresolved.
