NCS TELCO+ LEAVE POLICY
Document ID: NCS-HR-POL-01 | Version: 2.3 | Effective Date: 1 April 2026
Classification: Internal HR Policy (Training Sample Data)

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the types of leave available to all NCS Telco+ employees,
the entitlement for each, and the process for applying.

2. TYPES OF LEAVE AND ANNUAL ENTITLEMENT

2.1 Casual Leave (CL)
Every employee is entitled to 12 casual leave days per calendar year, credited
at 1 day per completed month of service. Casual leave cannot be carried forward
to the next year and cannot be encashed. A maximum of 3 consecutive casual
leave days may be taken at a time without special approval from the reporting
manager.

2.2 Sick Leave (SL)
Employees receive 10 sick leave days per calendar year. Sick leave for more
than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate to be submitted to HR
within 3 working days of returning to work. Unused sick leave may be carried
forward up to a maximum of 15 days.

2.3 Earned Leave (EL) / Privilege Leave
Employees accrue 1.5 days of earned leave per completed month of service
(18 days per year). Earned leave must be applied for at least 5 working days
in advance through the HR portal and requires reporting manager approval.
Up to 30 days of earned leave may be carried forward to the following year;
any balance beyond that is lapsed at year-end unless a written exception is
approved by the HR Head.

2.4 Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is granted for 26 weeks as per the Maternity Benefit Act,
applicable to the first two children. Paternity leave is granted for 2 weeks,
to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.

3. HOW TO APPLY
All leave (except emergency sick leave) must be applied through the HR portal
at least 2 working days in advance. Emergency sick leave should be
communicated to the reporting manager by phone or message on the same day,
with the formal portal entry completed within 48 hours.

4. UNAUTHORISED ABSENCE
Any absence not covered by an approved leave application or without prior
communication to the reporting manager for more than 2 consecutive working
days will be treated as unauthorised absence and may be subject to
disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct policy.

5. CONTACT
For any leave-related queries not covered here, employees should contact the
HR Helpdesk at hr.helpdesk@ncstelcoplus-sample.com (fictional address for
training purposes).
