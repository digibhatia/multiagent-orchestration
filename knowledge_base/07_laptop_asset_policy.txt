NCS TELCO+ LAPTOP & ASSET POLICY
Document ID: NCS-IT-POL-07 | Version: 1.2 | Effective Date: 1 May 2026
Classification: Internal IT Policy (Training Sample Data)

1. ELIGIBILITY AND REFRESH
1.1 Every full-time employee receives one company laptop on joining.
1.2 Laptops are refreshed every 4 years, or earlier if the device fails
hardware diagnostics. Developers may request a higher-spec model with
manager approval.

2. LOSS, THEFT AND DAMAGE
2.1 A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to the IT helpdesk and the
security team within 24 hours. A police complaint is required for theft.
2.2 Accidental damage is covered once per refresh cycle. A second incident
may result in a recovery charge decided by the department head.

3. DATA AND ENCRYPTION
3.1 All laptops use full-disk encryption. Disabling it is a policy violation.
3.2 Client confidential data must not be copied to personal USB drives or
personal cloud storage.

4. RETURN ON EXIT
4.1 All assets must be returned on or before the last working day. Final
settlement is released only after the IT clearance is complete.
