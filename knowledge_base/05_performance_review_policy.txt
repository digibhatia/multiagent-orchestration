NCS TELCO+ PERFORMANCE REVIEW AND PROMOTION POLICY
Document ID: NCS-HR-POL-05 | Version: 1.6 | Effective Date: 1 April 2026
Classification: Internal HR Policy (Training Sample Data)

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the performance review cycle, rating scale, and
promotion decision process for all NCS Telco+ employees.

2. REVIEW CYCLE FREQUENCY
Formal performance reviews are conducted twice a year: a Mid-Year Review
in September, and an Annual Review in March. In addition, employees within
their first 6 months of joining receive an informal 90-day check-in with
their reporting manager, which does not affect their formal rating.

3. RATING SCALE
Employees are rated on a 5-point scale at each Annual Review: Needs
Improvement, Developing, Meets Expectations, Exceeds Expectations, and
Outstanding. The Mid-Year Review is a lighter, developmental check-in and
does not assign a formal numeric or category rating.

4. WHO CONDUCTS THE REVIEW
The reporting manager conducts the primary review, informed by 360-degree
feedback collected from at least 2 peers and, where applicable, client
stakeholders. The final rating is calibrated in a cross-team Calibration
Committee to ensure consistency across similar roles before being shared
with the employee.

5. PROMOTION DECISION PROCESS
Promotions are considered once a year, at the Annual Review cycle only.
An employee becomes eligible for promotion consideration after receiving
"Exceeds Expectations" or "Outstanding" ratings in at least 2 of the last
3 review cycles, AND completing a minimum of 18 months in their current
role. The final promotion decision is made by the Department Head, based
on the reporting manager's recommendation and the Calibration Committee's
review, not by the reporting manager alone.

6. COMPENSATION REVIEW
Salary revisions are linked to the Annual Review cycle and are typically
communicated within 30 days of the Annual Review being finalised.
Mid-Year Reviews do not include a compensation change, except in cases of
an approved market-correction adjustment initiated by HR.

7. DISPUTING A RATING
An employee who disagrees with their rating may request a review meeting
with their HR Business Partner within 10 working days of the rating being
shared. The HR Business Partner will facilitate a discussion with the
reporting manager and, if unresolved, escalate to the Department Head for
a final decision.

8. CONTACT
For questions about the review cycle or promotion eligibility, contact your
HR Business Partner.
